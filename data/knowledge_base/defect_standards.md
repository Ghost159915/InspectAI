# Industrial Surface Defect Classification Standards

## Scratch
**Definition:** Linear surface damage caused by abrasion or mechanical contact.
**Severity guidelines:**
- Low: Surface scratch, depth < 0.1mm, does not affect structural integrity
- Medium: Deep scratch, visible cross-section deformation, may affect coating adhesion
- High: Through-material scratch, compromises structural integrity or sealing
**Recommended actions:**
- Low: Document and monitor; no immediate action required
- Medium: Flag for quality review; apply surface treatment if in visible area
- High: Remove from line; escalate to engineering for structural assessment

## Pit / Pitting
**Definition:** Small cavities or depressions in the surface, often caused by corrosion or impact.
**Severity guidelines:**
- Low: Isolated pits < 0.5mm diameter, cosmetic only
- Medium: Cluster of pits or diameter 0.5–2mm, may affect surface treatment adhesion
- High: Pits > 2mm, deep cavities, evidence of subsurface corrosion
**Recommended actions:**
- Low: Monitor; re-inspect at next scheduled cycle
- Medium: Apply protective coating; record location for trend tracking
- High: Remove from service; send for metallurgical analysis

## Crack
**Definition:** Fracture line in the material surface, can propagate under stress.
**Severity guidelines:**
- Low: Hairline crack < 1mm, surface layer only
- Medium: Crack 1–5mm, penetrates coating into base material
- High: Any crack > 5mm, through-thickness crack, or crack in load-bearing area
**Recommended actions:**
- Low: Mark and monitor; do not allow to progress to load-bearing zones
- Medium: Halt production on affected unit; conduct dye-penetrant inspection
- High: Immediate removal from service; mandatory engineering sign-off before re-use

## Contamination / Inclusion
**Definition:** Foreign material embedded in or on the surface (oil, debris, oxide inclusions).
**Severity guidelines:**
- Low: Surface contamination, cleanable
- Medium: Embedded contamination affecting coating adhesion or finish
- High: Subsurface inclusion affecting material properties
**Recommended actions:**
- Low: Clean with approved solvent; re-inspect
- Medium: Mechanical removal followed by surface treatment; document
- High: Reject component; trace contamination source; conduct root cause analysis

## Dent / Deformation
**Definition:** Localised plastic deformation of the surface without material removal.
**Severity guidelines:**
- Low: Cosmetic dent, depth < 0.2mm, non-functional area
- Medium: Dent in functional area or depth 0.2–1mm affecting fit/form
- High: Dent causing dimensional non-conformance or in sealing/mating surface
**Recommended actions:**
- Low: Cosmetic repair optional; document
- Medium: Dimensional re-check against drawing; rework if within tolerance
- High: Reject; evaluate for straightening or replacement
